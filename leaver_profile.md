## Role description
Runs the monthly payroll close for Dewaele-Mortier Transport NV (drivers national/international,
warehouse, admin, interim). Exports the hours from the planning tool, cleans them with his own
Excel macro, runs the monthly control, applies premiums and allowances, uploads the payroll file
to the provider portal before the cut-off and handles corrections. Also prepares the year-end close
and keeps the tariff sheet up to date with the yearly index.
