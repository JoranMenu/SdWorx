# Holiday pay for white-collar employees

- Single holiday pay is paid with the salary during the holiday.
- Double holiday pay (92% of monthly gross) is paid in May or June, depending on the client setting (DHP flag).
- For leavers, calculate the exit holiday pay (vertrekvakantiegeld) and issue the holiday certificate (VA-attest).
- For clients on the ZIV-flow, the sick days in the reference year must be manually corrected with the ZIV-corr sheet before the DHP run.
- Big clients (Wave 1) get the DHP simulation 2 weeks in advance.
